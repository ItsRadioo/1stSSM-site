About Beavers

The Beavers program gives youth age-appropriate opportunities to learn by doing, build friendships and participate in active Scouting experiences.

Program highlights

  • Games, crafts and introductory outdoor experiences
  • Learning to work with others and take part in a group
  • Simple challenges that build confidence and independence
