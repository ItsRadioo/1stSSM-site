About our group

Prepared for life

Learn how local Scouting builds confidence, practical skills, friendship and leadership through hands-on experiences.

Welcome to 1st Sault Ste. Marie

Our group provides age-appropriate Scouting experiences where youth learn by doing, take increasing responsibility and build the skills needed for life.

Our approach

Programs combine outdoor activity, teamwork, service, reflection and youth leadership. Volunteers create a safe and supportive environment while youth help shape their own adventures.

Our community

We are proud to serve families in Sault Ste. Marie and to build connections among youth, volunteers and community supporters.
