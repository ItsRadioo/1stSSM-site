Trail Ready Program

Gear today. Adventure tomorrow.

A proposed gear drive, lending and rental initiative designed to reduce equipment barriers for youth and families.

About the program

Reliable gear should not be a barrier

The Trail Ready Program helps families access suitable outdoor equipment for camps, hikes and Scouting adventures through donated gear, group-owned inventory and affordable lending or rental options.

C

Confidence

Reliable equipment helps youth participate confidently.

A

Adventure

Proper gear opens the door to new outdoor experiences.

M

Mentorship

Volunteers help families select and care for equipment.

P

Preparedness

The right equipment supports safe and comfortable outings.

Equipment donations

Give unused gear a new adventure

Clean, safe and serviceable outdoor equipment may be accepted to support the program.

  • Sleeping bags and pads

  • Snowshoes

  • Camp kitchen gear

Donation acceptance

Items may be declined because of condition, safety, cleanliness, missing components or storage limitations.
